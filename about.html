OUR STORY

Rooted in tradition. Made for your moments.

Nabban Bhai Zari Wala celebrates the beauty of Indian ethnic wear and the artistry of traditional embellishment.

A love for detail

Based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, Nabban Bhai Zari Wala brings together rich fabrics, intricate embroidery and thoughtful finishing. Our collection is created for celebrations, family gatherings, festive occasions and the moments you want to remember.

From elegant unstitched suit sets to statement pieces featuring zari, resham, dabka, katdana and other decorative details, every design is selected with an appreciation for the craft and character of Indian occasion wear.

Our approach

  • Traditional inspiration: designs that celebrate the colours, textures and embellishment of Indian attire.
  • Product clarity: clear descriptions of fabric, included components and visible work details.
  • Personal assistance: a helpful point of contact for questions about products and orders.
